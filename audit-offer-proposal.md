## Pricing rationale

€490 works as an accessible entry if the audit takes roughly four to five hours total. €2,400 supports about twenty to twenty-four hours for the complete package. These imply roughly €98–€123 per delivery hour before overhead and taxes; these are planning assumptions, not established operating data. Track actual time on the first projects before expanding scope.
